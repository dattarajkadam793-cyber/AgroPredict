🌾 SUGARCANE BASAL FERTILIZER MANAGEMENT 🌾
📌 UNDERSTANDING BASAL DOSE
Basal fertilizer refers to the first and most important application of nutrients given to the soil before planting or at the time of planting sugarcane setts or seedlings.

🎯 PURPOSE OF BASAL DOSE
✅ Supplies nutrients for early growth
✅ Promotes root establishment
✅ Improves sprouting & tillering 		✅ Enhances nutrient uptake efficiency
✅ Reduces early nutrient stress
✅ Improves final yield & quality
🔬 MACRONUTRIENTS REQUIRED
Nitrogen N
Supports vegetative growth and increases leaf area and biomass.
Phosphorus P
Essential for root development and energy transfer during early growth.
Potassium K
Strengthens stalks, improves sugar accumulation and stress tolerance.
Calcium Ca
Improves soil structure and supports root development and nutrient uptake.
Magnesium Mg
Central component of chlorophyll, essential for photosynthesis.
Sulphur S
Required for protein synthesis and improves nitrogen use efficiency.

📝 TIMING & APPLICATION METHOD
WHEN
Ideal Timing:
• 2-3 weeks before planting, OR
• At planting time

Why? Mixes nutrients with soil, prevents burn, ensures availability during germination.
WHERE
Placement Method:
• Apply in furrows or beds
• Place near root zone
• Mix lightly with soil
• Keep distance from setts
HOW
Application Steps:
• Conduct soil testing
• Calculate requirement
• Moisten soil if dry
• Apply uniformly in furrows
• Mix gently & plant setts
• Apply light irrigation
⚠️ IMPORTANT PRECAUTIONS
❌ DON'Ts:

• Don't apply on dry soil
• Don't let fertilizer touch buds

📅 IRRIGATION SCHEDULING

🌱 GERMINATION
Every 5–7 days
Provide pre-sowing irrigation 2–3 weeks before or at the time of sowing. During germination, light and frequent irrigation is essential. The soil should remain moist to support bud sprouting. Excess water must be avoided to prevent rotting of setts.
🌿 TILLERING
Every 6–10 days
This stage requires moderate and timely irrigation. Adequate water promotes the formation of healthy tillers. Water stress during this stage reduces cane population. Proper moisture improves nutrient uptake.
🚀 GRAND GROWTH
Every 7–12 days
This is the most critical stage with highest water demand. Rapid elongation of stalks occurs during this period. Moisture stress can significantly reduce yield. Regular monitoring of soil moisture is essential.
🍬 MATURATION
Every 12–15 days
Irrigation frequency is reduced during maturation stage. Limited water helps in accumulation of sugar in canes. Excess irrigation delays ripening and lowers quality. Proper scheduling improves cane sweetness.

⚠️ IRRIGATION PRECAUTIONS

❌ AVOID
• Over-irrigation (waterlogging)
• Hot afternoon watering
• Irregular schedules
• Using saline water
• Excess water in ripening
✅ FOLLOW
• Irrigate in early morning
• Monitor soil moisture
• Maintain consistent schedule
• Use quality water
• Reduce water during ripening

🌱 SOWING METHODS

THERE ARE TWO TYPES OF SUGARCANE SOWING METHODS

🌾 NORMAL SOWING

• Traditional method of sugarcane planting

• Setts are directly planted in the main field

• Requires proper spacing and irrigation

• Most commonly used method by farmers

🌱 NURSERY SOWING

• Modern approach with nursery preparation

• Healthy seedlings are later transplanted

• Saves seed material significantly

• Improves yield and uniformity
