(Making Farmland Ready)

🧹 Step 2 : Hoeing / Weeding
💧 Step 3 : Furrow Making System

✖
Why:
  • To loosen hard and compact soil.
  • To prepare land for sowing seeds.
  • To remove weeds and unwanted plants.
  • To mix crop residues and organic matter with soil.
  • To expose harmful insects and pests.
How:
  • Soil is turned and broken using a plough.
  • Traditional ploughs are drawn by bullocks.
  • Modern ploughing is done using tractors.
  • Cultivators and disc ploughs are also used.
  • Ploughing is done before sowing.
Importance:
  • Improves soil aeration and water-holding capacity.
  • Helps roots grow deeper and stronger.
  • Enhances soil fertility and nutrient availability.
  • Controls weeds and pests.
  • Increases crop yield and productivity.

Working of Ploughing

▶
Why:
  • To remove weeds competing with crops.
  • To reduce competition for nutrients and water.
  • To break hard crust on top soil.
  • To conserve soil moisture.
  • To improve crop growth.
How:
  • Weeds are uprooted using a hoe.
  • Top soil is loosened during hoeing.
  • Hand tools are used in small fields.
  • Mechanical weeders are used in large fields.
  • Hoeing is done at regular intervals.
Importance:
  • Improves soil aeration and root respiration.
  • Reduces weed population effectively.
  • Helps better absorption of water and nutrients.
  • Promotes healthy plant growth.
  • Increases crop yield.

Modern Method

▶
Why:
  • To create proper rows for planting.
  • To ensure efficient irrigation.
  • To maintain proper spacing between plants.
  • To prevent waterlogging.
  • To improve crop management.
How:
  • Furrows are formed using a ridger or plough.
  • Tractors or bullocks are used for operation.
  • Soil is pushed aside to form ridges and furrows.
  • Crops are planted on ridges or in furrows.
  • Furrows are made before sowing or planting.
Importance:
  • Ensures uniform distribution of irrigation water.
  • Provides proper drainage of excess water.
  • Improves root development and nutrient uptake.
  • Useful for crops like sugarcane and vegetables.
  • Increases crop productivity and yield.
