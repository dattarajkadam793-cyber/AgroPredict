Purpose of basal dose

🎯 Why apply basal dose?

  • Supplies nutrients for early growth
  • Promotes root establishment
  • Improves sprouting & tillering
  • Enhances nutrient uptake efficiency
  • Reduces early nutrient stress
  • Improves final yield & quality

Timing & method

🕐 When to apply

2–3 weeks before planting or at planting time. Mixes nutrients with soil, prevents burn, ensures availability during germination.

📍 Where to apply

  • Apply in furrows or beds
  • Place near root zone
  • Mix lightly with soil
  • Keep distance from setts

⚙️ How to apply

  • Conduct soil testing first
  • Moisten soil if dry
  • Apply uniformly in furrows
  • Mix gently & plant setts
  • Apply light irrigation after
Precautions

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't apply on dry soil
  • Don't let fertilizer touch buds
  • Don't apply excess nitrogen
  • Don't ignore soil testing
  • Don't apply in waterlogged conditions

✅ Do's

  • Apply on moist soil
  • Mix fertilizer properly with soil
  • Place near root zone
  • Follow soil test recommendations
  • Ensure uniform distribution

🎯 Benefits

  • Saves 40–60% water
  • Increases cane yield
  • Improves sugar content
  • Reduces labor costs
  • Ensures uniform growth

Scheduling tips

🌱 Germination & Tillering

Germination: Light, frequent irrigation. Keep soil moist. Avoid excess water to prevent sett rot.

Tillering: Moderate irrigation promotes healthy tillers. Water stress reduces cane population.

🚀 Grand Growth & Maturation

Grand Growth: Most critical stage — highest water demand. Moisture stress significantly reduces yield.

Maturation: Reduce irrigation. Limited water aids sugar accumulation and improves sweetness.

Irrigation method efficiency

🚿 Method comparison

💧 Flood irrigation30–40%  ⭐

☔ Sprinkler70–80%  ⭐⭐⭐
💧 Drip irrigation85–95%  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
💡 Use drip irrigation — saves 40–60% water!

📌 Method summary

Flood: Traditional, low cost, very low efficiency. Leads to waterlogging and nutrient leaching.

Furrow: Better control, moderate cost. Suitable for flat land.

Sprinkler: Good distribution, works on uneven terrain. Higher setup cost.

Drip: Best option. Delivers water directly to root zone. Maximum savings.

✅ Balanced approach

Irrigation precautions

❌ Avoid

  • Over-irrigation / waterlogging
  • Hot afternoon watering
  • Irregular schedules
  • Using saline water
  • Excess water during ripening

✅ Follow

  • Irrigate in early morning
  • Monitor soil moisture
  • Maintain consistent schedule
  • Use quality water
  • Reduce water during ripening

💡 Best Practices

  • Use soil moisture sensors if possible
  • Combine drip with fertigation
  • Inspect drip lines regularly
  • Keep drainage channels clear
  • Record irrigation dates
