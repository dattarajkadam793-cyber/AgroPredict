📋 Basic Stage Information

Grand Growth Stage — Key Facts

Duration

120 – 270 days after planting
(varies by variety & climate)

Key Characteristics

  • Rapid elongation of cane
  • Maximum internode development
  • Fast height increase
  • Highest leaf area index
  • Canopy closes & suppresses weeds

Physiological Importance

  • Contributes 70–75% of total yield
  • High photosynthetic activity
  • Maximum biomass accumulation
  • Tillering stabilizes

Water Requirement

  • Highest water demand period
  • Moisture stress severely reduces yield
  • Regular irrigation essential

Nutrient Requirement

  • Peak nutrient uptake stage
  • High requirement of N & K
  • Final split dose of nitrogen applied

Pest & Disease Incidence

  • Top borer
  • Internode borer
  • Pyrilla infestation common

Field Operations

  • Earthing up to support canes
  • Trash mulching for moisture conservation
  • Proper drainage during rainy season

⚠ Critical Importance

Stress at this stage causes irreversible yield loss. Directly affects cane length, weight, and final yield.

💧 Water Management

Grand Growth Stage

Water Requirement

Total: ~1000 mm (Grand Growth Stage)
Daily: 4 – 7 mm/day
Higher need in hot & dry weather

Factors affecting irrigation:

  • Soil type & water holding capacity
  • Temperature & humidity
  • Evapotranspiration (ET)
  • Irrigation method (drip saves water)

🌿 Diseases & Pests

Grand Growth Stage (4–7 Months After Planting)

Red RotSymptoms

Red colour inside cane with white patches. Leaves turn orange → yellow → dry. Alcohol smell.

Control

Use disease-free setts. Remove infected clumps. Avoid ratoon in infected field.

SmutSymptoms

Black whip-like structure from top. Thin weak tillers. Reduced cane growth.

Control

Plant healthy setts. Remove & burn infected whips. Grow resistant varieties.

WiltSymptoms

Yellowing & drying of leaves. Hollow stalk with reddish colour. Plant dries slowly.

Control

Avoid wilt-sick soil. Remove infected stubbles. Use healthy planting material.

Pokkah BoengSymptoms

Yellow patches on leaves. Leaves twist & wrinkle. Top rot in severe cases.

Control

Remove infected tops. Maintain field sanitation. Spray recommended fungicide.

MosaicSymptoms

Yellow & green stripes on leaves. Stunted plant growth. Weak stalk formation.

Control

Use virus-free setts. Control aphids. Remove infected plants.

Grassy ShootSymptoms

Many thin grassy tillers. Pale yellow leaves. No proper cane formation.

Control

Use disease-free setts. Remove infected clumps. Hot water treatment of setts.

Ratoon StuntingSymptoms

Stunted plant height. Thin stalks. Poor tiller formation.

Control

Use clean seed cane. Sterilize cutting tools. Avoid infected ratoon crop.

Leaf ScaldSymptoms

White streaks on leaves. Yellow leaf margins. Leaf drying in severe cases.

Control

Use disease-free material. Remove infected plants. Grow resistant varieties.

Sugarcane BorersSymptoms

Dead heart in young plants. Bore holes with frass. Bunchy top in mature cane.

Control

Use resistant varieties. Release Trichogramma. Apply recommended insecticide.

🌱 Weeding in Grand Growth Stage

Methods, Advantages & Management

Manual Weeding

Hand hoe (khurpi) or spade between rows. Suitable for small fields & low weed density.

✔ Advantages:
No chemical damage, safe for crop & soil
✘ Disadvantages:
Labour-intensive, time-consuming, costly for large fields
Mechanical Weeding

Power weeder / tractor-mounted weeder. Removes weeds between rows & loosens soil.

✔ Advantages:
Fast, effective, saves labour, suitable for large fields
✘ Disadvantages:
Can't remove weeds near base; risk of root damage
Chemical Weeding

Post-emergence herbicides for heavy weed infestation. Needs correct dose & timing.

✔ Advantages:
Saves time & labour, effective for heavy infestation
✘ Disadvantages:
Crop injury risk, environmental pollution, precise use needed

🌾 Mothi Bharani Guide

Second Major Fertilizer Stage

What is Mothi Bharani?

  • Second & major fertilizer application stage
  • Tiller count already fixed
  • Rapid vegetative growth phase
  • High nutrient demand period

⏱ Timing & Conditions

  • 75 – 120 days after planting
  • Healthy & uniform tillers present
  • Moist soil condition required

Application Method

  2. Cover properly with soil (earthing-up)
  3. Give light irrigation after application

Iron

Magnesium

  • Avoid excess nitrogen
  • Prevent water stagnation after application
  • Do not delay application
