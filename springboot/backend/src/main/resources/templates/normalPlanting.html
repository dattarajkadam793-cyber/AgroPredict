📖 Basic Planting Information

Planting Time

Plant sugarcane between November and January.
Special methods allow planting in August–September.
Avoid planting during heavy rain or when the field has standing water.

Soil & Climate

Use deep, fertile, well-drained soil. Clay loam or black soil is suitable in Kolhapur.
Ensure warm climate and sufficient water throughout the crop period.

Varieties (Types)

Grow 86 varieties. Always select disease-free and healthy seed cane.
Get approved varieties from your sugar factory or agriculture office.

Spacing & Planting

Maintain 75–90 cm row-to-row spacing.
Use 2–3 bud setts for planting. Place setts in furrows and cover lightly with soil.

Water / Irrigation

Provide regular irrigation. Avoid over-irrigation and water stagnation.
Use drip irrigation to save water and improve yield.

Fertilizer & Manure

Apply FYM (shekhar khat) before planting.
Use Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potash as recommended in 2–3 split doses.

Ratoon Crop

Allow the crop to regrow from old stubble after harvest.
With good care, take 2–3 ratoon crops.

Important Tips

Always use healthy, good-quality seed cane. Do soil testing before deciding fertilizer dose.
Keep the field weed-free during early growth.

⚗️ Planting & Medicine

Planting Steps

Cut sugarcane into parts — each part should have 3 buds.
Lay sugarcane horizontally in the furrow with eyes/buds facing upward.

Medicine / Herbicide

Apply Goal Medicine or any approved herbicide on the farmland on the 2nd day after sowing.
This controls weeds and protects early germination.
